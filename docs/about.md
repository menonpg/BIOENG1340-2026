Course materials and coursework for **BIOENG 1340/2340 (Fall 2026)**: medical image processing and analysis using MATLAB, Python (SimpleITK), and 3D visualization tools (ITK-SNAP, ParaView).

**Instructor:** Prahlad Menon, PhD, PMP

## Course arc

- **Week01** — Loading DICOM Data & First Scripts
- **Week02** — Least-Squares Fitting & VTK Volume Export
- **Week03** — Image Filtering & Convolution as a Linear Operator
- **Week04** — SNR & CNR
- **Week05** — MR Physics: Larmor, Bloch Equations & Slice Selection
- **Week06** — MRI Image Formation: Quiz Solutions
- **Week07** — Spatial Localization, Pulse Sequences & k-space
- **Week08** — X-ray & CT Image Formation
- **Week09** — Algebraic CT Reconstruction & Study Design
- **Week10** — CT Image Formation, Reconstruction & Artifacts

## Tools

- **Python** (NumPy, SciPy, scikit-image, SimpleITK, matplotlib) in Jupyter notebooks
- **MATLAB** for DICOM I/O, filtering, and VTK export
- **ITK-SNAP** (segmentation) and **ParaView** (3-D visualization)
